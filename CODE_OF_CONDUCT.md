# Code of conduct

We want participation in Yinjiao Bangbang to be safe, respectful, and useful.

Contributors and maintainers must:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on the work;
- welcome questions from people with different experience levels;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted disclosure of personal information;
- protect student, educator, and contributor privacy;
- disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a technical or moderation decision.

Unacceptable behavior may be removed, and repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions. Report conduct concerns privately to the primary maintainer through the GitHub profile. The maintainer will review reports as confidentially as practical and will avoid conflicts of interest when handling them.
